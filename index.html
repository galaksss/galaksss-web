Work · 01

OneDay

A web app that shows how much you really worked on your goals. Product, design, code and the landing page — one person, three weeks.

Open the app ↗ See the landing ↗

Pass 01 · Goals

Every goal counts its days

Four kinds of goals: by time, by amount, check-ins and days without a slip. Each one counts from day one, the week fits on one screen, and a planned day off never breaks a streak.

Pass 02 · Focus

A focus mode made to be filmed

A full-screen stopwatch for timelapses. The glow behind the digits grows with the hours you put in; Space or a click pauses it, and the screen dims when you look away.

Pass 03 · History

Progress you read at a glance

A week or 30 days in one card: bars in your goals' colors, Day N, streak and best day. Hover a day for the breakdown, click it to open that day's review.

Pass 04 · Day card

A day you want to share

The evening wrap-up becomes a card for stories or the feed. Every shared card is an ad for the product, built into the product.

Pass 05 · Landing

A landing that shows instead of tells

Built from live copies of the app's screens, not screenshots: a running stopwatch, goal cards that play by themselves, an encryption demo. English and Russian.

02ConceptLanding page

Hour

A scroll story for an imaginary color-grading app. One shader draws a lake from dawn to night, and the grade follows the light as you scroll — no photos, no video.

WebGL · GLSL · scroll-driven

03ConceptProduct page

Orrin

An Apple-style page for an imaginary speaker. No 3D model — the speaker is ray-marched in a shader: it turns, opens up into its parts and changes finish as you scroll.

WebGL · ray marching · SDF

04ConceptStudio site

A site for an imaginary VFX studio. Sixty thousand particles become a sphere, a wave, the studio's name, a knot and a galaxy as you scroll.

WebGL · 60k particles · GLSL

05Personal projectWeb app

Beatgrid

Frame-exact beat markers for editors. Tap the tempo or drop a track: it estimates the BPM, draws the waveform and shows on which frame every beat lands.

Web Audio · Canvas · Vite
