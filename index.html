Work · 01

OneDay

A web app that shows how much you really worked on your goals. Product, design, code and the landing page — one person, two weeks.

Open the app ↗ See the landing ↗

  1. 9:30 AM1/4

    Start the stopwatch

    One tap starts the stopwatch on a goal card. A swim is one more tap, videos count in pieces — and a planned rest day never breaks the streak. Space pauses, and reloading the page doesn't lose a second.

  2. 11:00 AM2/4

    Work in focus mode

    A full-screen stopwatch made to be filmed as a timelapse. The glow behind the digits grows with the hours you put in.

  3. 8:15 PM3/4

    See the week add up

    History turns the hours into bars in your goals' colors, with Day N, the streak and the best day. Hover a day for its breakdown, click it to open that day's card.

  4. 10:40 PM4/4

    Wrap up the day

    Hours and check-ins per goal, and a card for stories or the feed. Every shared card is an ad for the product, built into the product.
