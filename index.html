Case study

        
OneDay

        
A web app that shows how much you really worked on your goals. Product, design, code and the landing page — one person, three weeks.

        

          Open the app ↗
          See the landing ↗
        

      


      
      

        

          

            01
            
Every goal counts its days

            
By time, by amount, with check-ins or as days without a slip. Each goal counts from day one, and the week fits on one screen.

            
          

          

            02
            
A focus mode made to be filmed

            
A full-screen stopwatch with a chime at the daily goal. It pauses on space, dims when you look away and protects OLED screens.

            
          

          

            03
            
A day you want to share

            
The evening wrap-up becomes a card for stories or the feed. Every shared card is an ad for the product, built into the product.

            
          

          

            04
            
A landing that shows instead of tells

            
Live previews of the real app instead of screenshots: the timer and the day card on the page are working components. English and Russian.
