How it works

One frame.
Every light.

01

A day in a shader

The landscape isn't a photo or a video. Sky, sun, stars, three mountain ridges and the lake are drawn by one fragment shader, sixty times a second.

02

Looks that follow the light

Every hour has its own grade — split toning, contrast, saturation — and the page blends between them as you scroll, like keyframes on a timeline.

03

Nothing to download

No images, no video files. The whole page weighs less than a single photo; the shader draws at three-quarter resolution and stops when you scroll past it.
