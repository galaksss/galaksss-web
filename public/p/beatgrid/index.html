Why frames,
not seconds

01

Frame-exact

At 128 BPM and 24 fps a beat lasts 11.25 frames. Round every beat the same way and the cuts drift off the music. Beatgrid shows the 11 · 11 · 11 · 12 pattern that keeps them on it.

02

Your track under the grid

Drop a song to see its waveform. Beatgrid estimates the tempo and the first beat; nudge the offset until the lines sit on the hits.

03

Markers out

Copy the timecodes or download a CSV with frames, bars and timecodes, and place markers in any editor.
